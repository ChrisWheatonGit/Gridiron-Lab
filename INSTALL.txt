Gridiron Lab Schedule -> Statistics Sync

Back up src/app/schedule/page.tsx and src/app/stats before installing.
Copy the src folder into your project root, merge and replace these three files.
Run npm.cmd run dev.
Schedule scores and dates are the source for game results, records and points in Statistics Studio.
Statistics player entries and advanced manual team totals remain editable and preserved.
Existing browser localStorage keys stay unchanged.
Use View [team] season statistics on Schedule Builder or choose the team and year in Stats.
